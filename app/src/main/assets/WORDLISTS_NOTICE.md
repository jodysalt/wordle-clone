# Word list notice

- `valid_guesses.txt` is the five-letter subset (exactly five lowercase ASCII
  letters, sorted, de-duplicated) of the ENABLE word list, downloaded from
  <https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dolph/dictionary/master/enable1.txt>.
  The ENABLE word list is in the public domain.
- `answers.txt` is a hand-picked subset of common, familiar words from that
  same list, and is likewise in the public domain.

Neither list is derived from the Wordle or New York Times answer or guess lists.
